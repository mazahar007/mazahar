FAQ
Questions, answered

      

      

        
Do you only edit, or do you handle strategy too?
Both. Editing alone rarely grows a channel. I combine production with title/thumbnail strategy, SEO and a content plan — so each video has a clear job to do.

        
Can you really help me reach US audiences?
That's my specialty. It comes down to topic selection, hooks in the first 30 seconds, US-English titles, pacing and SEO signals. We build the plan around US viewers from day one.

        
How fast is turnaround?
It depends on volume and complexity — Shorts are fastest, long-form motion-graphics videos take longer. Your timeline is confirmed in the quote before we start, and Growth/Scale clients get priority.

        
Do I need to show my face on camera?
Not necessarily. Many channels I help are faceless — AI voice/visuals, stock + motion graphics, or screen-based formats. We'll pick the format that fits you and your niche.

        
How is pricing structured?
Pricing depends on the package — Launch, Growth, or Scale & Monetize — and the scope of your project. Every engagement is scoped after a short discovery chat, so send your brief and I'll reply with a custom quote. No hidden fees.
